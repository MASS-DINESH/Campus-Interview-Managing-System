Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are mandatory for placement application?

You must provide your passport photograph, 10th marksheet, 12th/Diploma marksheet, college semester transcripts, and your latest professional resume in PDF/DOC format.

How long does the admin review process take?

Admin verification typically takes 24 to 48 business hours. You can track your application status live on your candidate dashboard.

Can I update my documents after submitting an application?

If your application is pending review, you can submit a support ticket below to request document correction or upload a fresh application form.
