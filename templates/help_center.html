Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a job application?
To create a job application, navigate to the "Job Request" page from the main menu. Fill out all required fields including your personal information, qualifications, and upload the necessary documents. Once completed, click the "Submit Application" button.
What documents do I need to submit with my application?
You'll need to upload a passport-sized photo, 10th and 12th mark sheets, your resume, and college mark sheets. All documents should be in PDF format except the photo which can be JPG or PNG.
How can I check the status of my application?
You can check your application status by visiting the "My Applications" page. This page shows all your submitted applications and their current status (Pending, Accepted, or Declined).
What should I do if I forget my password?
If you've forgotten your password, please contact the system administrator at support@cims.com. They will assist you in resetting your password and regaining access to your account.
How long does it take to process an application?
Application processing times vary depending on the company and position. Typically, you can expect to hear back within 2-4 weeks. You'll receive a notification when your application status changes.
